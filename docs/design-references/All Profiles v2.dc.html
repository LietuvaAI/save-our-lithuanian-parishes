Every parish, across the whole record

This is the institution view — every Lithuanian parish, mission, and congregation as a community in its own right, not a building. Each is drawn as a life on one shared timeline that runs from the 1880s, when the first congregations were founded by immigrant communities, to the present day: the bar marks the years the institution existed, colored by what became of it, and left open where the community still lives. Use it to find a parish by name, place, or era and open its full profile. For the physical churches — which a parish may have moved through more than one of — see the building view; for how the whole population changed over time and where each parish ended up, see Parish & Mission Outcomes.
