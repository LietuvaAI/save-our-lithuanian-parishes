## draugas-links.json

- **Generated by:** `scripts/verify-draugas-links.mjs`, which probes the direct Draugas PDF URL variants for every citation date in `parishes.json` (~1 request/second) and records each date as `verified` (public PDF, 200), `gated` (exists but subscriber-only, 401), or `unresolved`.
- Committed like the other generated data files; re-run the script to refresh. Verified/gated entries are kept on re-runs, unresolved dates are re-probed.
- Parish profile pages link a citation only when the cache carries a verified or subscriber-gated issue-level URL. An unresolved date is rendered as not yet linked; a per-year archive page is never presented as the cited issue. This file affects **links only** — no figures are derived from it.

## draugas-mention-index.json

- **Canonical source:** `culturenet-brain` → `docs/research/draugas-parish-registry-1909-2007/mention-index.json`, generated deterministically from the T9 Stage-1 hit shards under the current gazetteer.
- **Unit:** `registry_hit_occurrences` counts attributed `parapij` stem-hit occurrences; `unique_dated_issues` counts distinct issue-date and source-file pairs. They are not interchangeable.
- **Public role:** powers `/parishes/<slug>/draugas` for profiles with more than 10 attributed hits. Displayed totals are re-derived from issue rows, and exact links are joined only through `draugas-links.json` when the verified URL filename matches the source filename.
- **Boundary:** this is an archive-navigation index, not a claim-level adjudication ledger. The T9 corpus and OCR cache are research infrastructure only; raw context and local corpus paths never publish. Every public issue link must resolve to the corresponding online `draugas.org` source. The index does not affect the public institution census, classifications, or lifecycle dates.

- **Authority:** Generated in `culturenet-brain` from the unified CultureNet parish canon and copied here as a release artifact. It is the sole authority for public U.S. institution membership, identity, type, class, canonical entity ID, and profile route.
