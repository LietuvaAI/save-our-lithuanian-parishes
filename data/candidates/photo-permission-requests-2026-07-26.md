Esu SaveOurLithuanianParishes.org — viešo advokacijos projekto, dokumentuojančio

neskelbsime. Mielai atsiųsiu pilną puslapių sąrašą ir parodysiu, kaip nuotraukos

Su pagarba,
Vilija

## Clean-source replacements to pursue in parallel (no permission needed)

- **HABS / Library of Congress** — Historic American Buildings Survey photographs
  are US-government public domain; several Pennsylvania anthracite-region churches
  are covered.
- **Pre-1931 US-published postcards and photographs** — public domain; parish
  centennial books and postcard archives cover most of the 1900s-built churches.
- **Wikimedia Commons** — CC-licensed church photos exist for several of these
  buildings; usable now with license-compliant attribution (`open_license`).
- **Own photography** — any parish Vilija or allies can reach with a camera is
  `own_work`, the cleanest class of all.
