# Research leads surfaced by the photo sweep (2026-07-27)

Identity questions the clean-source photo sweep raised in passing. None acted
on — photo agents ran on exhausted search budgets and these need the
adjudication lane's rigor. Sources are in the batch outputs referenced.

1. **sv-petro-cambridge-ma** — batch NE1 could not confirm a Lithuanian
   "St. Peter" parish in Cambridge at all; it identifies Cambridge's
   Lithuanian national parish as Immaculate Conception (Windsor St.), and
   reads our record as possibly a data mismatch with the South Boston
   St. Peter. Verify against Draugas/Wolkovich before touching the record.

2. **sv-kazimiero-chicago-il** — batch M1 cross-referenced two independent
   parish lists and found no Lithuanian St. Casimir parish within Chicago
   city limits — only Chicago Heights (distinct municipality). Possible city
   correction on the record.

3. **lietuviu-baznycia-kearny-kearny-nj** — batch M3: the 1915 Lithuanian
   parish building (St. Michael the Archangel) was in neighboring Harrison,
   NJ; Kearny proper got Our Lady of Sorrows only ~1955. City/succession
   nuance for the record.

4. **Cleveland photo cross-filing guard** — adjudicated (see photos.json
   _registryNotes): Sowinski Ave St. Casimir = Polish parish; never attach
   its images to the Lithuanian Cleveland records.
